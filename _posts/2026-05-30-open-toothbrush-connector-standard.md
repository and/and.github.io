When the bristles on a toothbrush wear out, we usually throw away the whole toothbrush. Most of the material is still fine.

This is a proposal, not a finished product. I started on it in 2022 and it is still open.

## From a product to a standard

My first thought was to design a modular toothbrush with replaceable parts. That would cut waste. After a while I realised a single product from one maker would have limited effect.

Most efforts to make oral care more sustainable are about materials: bamboo handles, compostable bristles. The real problem is structural. Each brand uses its own connector, which locks you in and makes it impossible to swap parts between brands. That is a systems problem, not a materials problem.

## The proposal

An open connector standard that any manufacturer could adopt, royalty-free. It is modelled on the EU's USB-C mandate. If the EU can require every phone to share a common charger, the same reasoning applies to toothbrush connectors.

## What I've done so far

- Sketched the idea in 2022.
- Read ISO 20126:2022, the standard for manual toothbrushes, and confirmed that it does not cover a connector that works across brands.
- Surveyed the patent landscape.
- In May 2026, wrote to AENOR, Spain's national standards body, to ask whether anyone has ever proposed such a standard.
