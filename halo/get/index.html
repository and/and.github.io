Halo

Someone wants you to know when they're busy.

You've been invited to Halo. Once you accept, their status shows next to their name in your contacts. You'll see they're driving or in a meeting before you call.

  • JD Jane Doe 🚗 DrivingUntil 6:30 PM
  • JS John Smith 📅 In a MeetingUntil 4:00 PM
  • EC Emily ClarkAvailable

Your contacts, with Halo. Names are examples.

Install

Android Get it on Google Play

Halo is in testing. Open the link with the Google account the person who invited you added.

iPhone

Through TestFlight, by invite for now. Ask the person who sent you this link to add you.

Then

  1. Open Halo and verify your phone number.
  2. Your invite is already waiting. Tap Accept.
  3. Choose what they see of your status: all of it, or only Busy.
