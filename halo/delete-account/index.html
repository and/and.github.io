Delete your Halo account

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Halo and tap Profile.
  • Tap Delete & Clear My Account Data, then confirm.

Halo deletes your account straight away. On your phone it also puts back the original names of contacts that showed a Halo status.

Without the app

Email anands.www.email@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Halo account" and the phone number you signed in with. We may send an SMS or ask you to reply from that number to confirm it's yours, then delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (your phone number)
  • Your status and the list of people allowed to see it
  • Invites you sent and invites sent to you
  • Push notification tokens for your devices
  • Any request for more contact slots
  • Your upcoming meeting times, if you turned on Calendar meetings

What may be kept

Feedback messages you sent from the app are kept to improve Halo. Ask in the same email if you want them deleted too. Your status on other people's phones disappears the next time their Halo app syncs.
