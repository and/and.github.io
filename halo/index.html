You call someone and it rings out. Later you find out they were driving. Or it's the other way round: you're in a meeting, your phone buzzes on the table, and you cut the call and hope they don't worry.

Nobody did anything wrong. The caller just couldn't tell.

Halo is a small app for Android and iPhone that fixes this. It lets your contacts know you're busy before they call.

What it does

Halo shows your status where people already look: your name in their phone's contacts.

When Priya starts driving, her name on your phone changes from Priya Sharma to Priya Sharma 🚗 Driving. You see it in your phone app, in your recent calls, on the incoming call screen, and in WhatsApp, which uses the names from your contacts. When she's free again, her name goes back to just Priya Sharma.

There's nothing new to open and nothing to check. You were about to call her anyway, and now you can see she's on the road.

These are the statuses you can pick:

  • 🟢 Available
  • 🚗 Driving
  • 📅 In a Meeting
  • 🌙 Do Not Disturb
  • 🏋️ At the Gym

Halo can also set some of them for you. Under Auto-set you can turn on Driving, Calendar meetings and Low battery, and Halo switches your status when it notices one of those. Each one is a switch, so you choose which ones run.

The app

You sign in with your phone number and a verification code. There's no username, password or profile to fill in.

After that it's one screen. At the top is your status. Below it is who sees it, split into Family and Work.

How sharing works

Nobody sees your status unless you've both said yes.

  1. Invite someone. Tap + Invite under Family or Work and pick them from your contacts. Halo opens your share sheet with a link, so you can send it however you like.
  2. They install Halo and sign in with the same number you picked.
  3. They accept. Your invite is waiting for them, with your name as it's saved in their contacts. They tap Accept and choose Family or Work for you.
  4. That's it. From then on, you each see the other's status on your own phones.

If they tap Ignore, the invite is deleted. You aren't told.

Family and Work

Not everyone needs to know you're at the gym.

  • Family sees your full status: 🚗 Driving, 📅 In a Meeting, 🏋️ At the Gym and so on.
  • Work sees only 🔴 Busy. Your colleague learns it's not a good time, and nothing more.

You can move someone between the two at any time.

Privacy

Halo is built to know as little about you as it can.

  • No names on the server. Names come from the contacts already on each phone. Halo's server never gets your contact list.
  • Your number isn't your ID. Your status is filed under a one-way hash of your phone number, not the number itself. An invite does carry your number, so the other person's phone can find you in their contacts.
  • Only people you've accepted. Your status can be read only by the people who've accepted an invite with you. Someone with a pending invite sees nothing yet.
  • Very little to share. What goes up is your current status, like 🚗 Driving, the time you set it, and a few app settings.
  • Your contacts are put back. To show a status, Halo edits the contact's name on your phone, so it asks for permission to edit contacts. It keeps the original name on your phone and restores it when the person is available again.
